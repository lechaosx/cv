# Product decisions

## English and Czech [Explicit]

Each language has a directly linkable CV and prints its own content. The homepage uses the saved choice or browser language, with English available when JavaScript is disabled.

## Screen preferences and print [Explicit]

Readers can use the system theme or remember a light/dark preference on narrow screens. Desktop and print keep the CV's mixed palette, and page controls stay out of the PDF.

## Responsive reading order [Explicit]

Small screens stack timeline details and move Connect after the career history. Longer tablet paragraphs keep a comfortable line length; desktop and print retain the sidebar layout. Phone, email and visible profile links are reachable by keyboard.

## Editable content [Explicit]

Pages CMS edits both translations together without a live editing interface. Dates accept years or month/year, ongoing roles stay open-ended, and completion dates can stand alone.

## Discovery and sharing [Explicit]

Search results and share previews follow the page language, with one identity across translations. All crawlers are allowed; the sitemap lists the two canonical CV pages.

## Readable exports [Explicit]

Each language has a Markdown CV; a bilingual full export and compact `llms.txt` index make the same facts available to text readers and assistants. All exports follow the edited content, without another document to maintain.
